About Project

KalaSetu

KalaSetu is an AI-driven market linkage platform for artisans, cooperatives, and craft clusters. It supports cataloging, multilingual voice-first input, transparent pricing, and direct connections to retail and institutional buyers.

The platform aligns with the SIH26090 challenge area and supports the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment by building a fair, data-supported pathway from handmade production to buyer discovery.
