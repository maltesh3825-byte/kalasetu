# KalaSetu — Artisan Market Linkage Platform

KalaSetu is a digital marketplace and cataloging platform designed for marginalized artisans, craft clusters, cooperatives, and rural micro-entrepreneurs to connect directly with buyers, institutions, and bulk demand networks.

The platform combines product publishing, image-based analysis, catalog generation, multilingual support, buyer inquiry, order tracking, and account workflows in a single application.

## Mission

KalaSetu aims to reduce dependency on middlemen and create a more transparent and inclusive digital commerce route for artisans. It helps artisans digitize inventory, prepare product listings, estimate pricing, create multilingual descriptions, and respond to buyer demand from retail and institutional channels.

## Key Features

- Product photo analysis and listing generation
- Artisan studio for uploading, reviewing, and publishing craft products
- Buyer marketplace for catalog browsing and order requests
- Bulk and institutional inquiry workflow
- Unified profile for buyer and seller workflows
- Wishlist, order history, request history, and account workspace
- Multilingual support across major Indian languages
- Direct WhatsApp buyer contact support
- Notification and account management experience
- Artisan profile and location-aware buyer discovery support
- Admin review and backend-managed institutional request workflows

## Target Users

- Artisans and craft makers
- Local cooperatives and artisan clusters
- Retail and institutional buyers
- Government and public-market linkage teams
- Buyers looking for handmade, rural, and traditional products

## Tech Stack

The project includes a FastAPI backend, SQLite data layer, static web frontend assets, and an Expo/React Native mobile application interface for Android and mobile distribution workflows.

## Outcome

KalaSetu creates a connected ecosystem where artisans can publish craft inventory, understand digital demand, respond to institutional and bulk opportunities, and participate in a fairer national marketplace with less reliance on intermediaries.
